Same as yesterday

One button repeats yesterday's value. A normal day is one tap per symptom, or one tap for all of them.

Colour says it first

Teal for fine, red for rough. You can read a whole week at a glance, before reading a single number.

Your own symptoms

Name them yourself. Each is either a strength from 1 to 10 or a plain yes/no.

Install it from this page's link.

No app store. It is a web app that installs like an app, and keeps working without a connection.

  1. Open Symmie in Chrome

    On your Android phone, tap Open the app below.

  2. Tap "Install"

    Chrome offers it at the bottom, or use the menu ⋮ → Install app.

  3. Set your lock

    Pick a username and password. They stay on this phone.
