Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is made for family use, with no ads and no store.

Which phones does it work on?

Android, in Chrome. It installs from a link and works without a connection.

What if I lose my phone?

The diary is only on the phone. Save a backup file now and then; restoring it on a new phone brings everything back.

Is the password real security?

It is a lock against someone borrowing your phone, not against someone who can read its storage. We say so in the app, too.

Does it give medical advice?

No. It shows descriptive patterns and nothing more. Show them to your doctor.
